How to Request Deletion

  1. Open the LineupSync Privacy & Deletion Form.
  2. Select the request type and tell us you want your LineupSync account and related personal data deleted.
  3. Include the email address associated with your account.
This page is provided as the public deletion-request path for the LineupSync Android listing. If you cannot email from your account address, include enough detail for us to verify ownership before we act.

What to Include in Your Request

Required

The email address associated with your LineupSync account, or enough information for us to locate it.

Helpful

Your display name, ensemble name, and whether you want full account deletion or help with a narrower privacy request.

If we need more information to verify the request, we may reply before deleting data.

What We Aim to Delete

  • Your LineupSync account profile information associated with the request.
  • Your availability defaults, personal preferences, and app-linked device tokens where applicable.
  • Your access to ensembles and related membership records that are tied to your account.
  • Other personal data that we control for operating LineupSync, where deletion is reasonably practical.

What We May Retain

In some situations, we may keep limited information after a deletion request.

  • Records we are required to keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or compliance reasons.
  • Minimal records needed to resolve disputes or protect the service and other users.
  • Information that has been de-identified so it no longer reasonably identifies you.

Where complete deletion is not practical, we will aim to de-identify retained data where reasonably possible.
