TakeLight turns Philips Hue into a studio recording light that responds to your DAW. Arm a track or hit record, and your selected lights update automatically. It is the classic door-light concept, rebuilt for modern home and project studios.

How TakeLight Works

TakeLight runs in the macOS menu bar and watches your configured DAW and MIDI signals. When armed or recording status changes, TakeLight updates your selected Hue lights in real time.

That gives you a clear "recording armed" and "recording in progress" signal in other rooms, helping reduce interruptions during takes. No more guessing if the 'tape' is rolling!

Current Feature Set

  • Automatic Hue Bridge discovery plus manual host entry fallback
  • Bridge pairing flow and persistent local bridge credentials
  • Selectable light targets (single room or multiple lights)
  • CoreMIDI-driven DAW status mapping and local light automation

Roadmap

TakeLight is intentionally focused today on local MIDI-to-Hue studio automation. Next milestones expand ecosystem support while keeping the local-first philosophy.
