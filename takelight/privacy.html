Privacy policy for the TakeLight app.

TakeLight is a macOS menu bar utility operated by Cello Hill. It can read local MIDI control-surface state and optionally control a user-selected Philips Hue Bridge on your local network. This policy explains what data is processed, where it stays, and your choices.

Last updated: June 7, 2026
No accounts required
No sale of personal data
Local-first operation

TakeLight does not require an account and does not run a user cloud backend for app data.

Local network access only when needed

If you enable Hue control, TakeLight sends commands only to your selected Hue Bridge on your local LAN.

Minimal data storage

The app stores Hue configuration and app preferences on your Mac so your setup persists between launches.

What We Collect

TakeLight is designed to minimize data collection. Most data processing happens locally on your Mac.

Hue bridge configuration

If you pair a Philips Hue Bridge, TakeLight stores the bridge host, application key, and selected light IDs locally on your device.

App preferences

The app may store local preferences, such as selected automation targets and mapping choices, so your setup persists.

MIDI and presence signals

TakeLight reads local MIDI/control-surface state to drive automation. These signals are processed in-app and are not sent to a TakeLight cloud service.

No account profile data

TakeLight does not require user accounts and does not collect profile fields like name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

Information stored on your device

  • Hue bridge host, bridge application key, and selected light IDs (if configured).
  • MIDI and automation mapping preferences.
  • General app behavior preferences required for menu bar operation.

Optional access and system prompts

  • Local network access may be requested when discovering or controlling a Hue Bridge.
  • MIDI device access is used to read local control-surface signals.
TakeLight does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking for targeted advertising.

How We Use Information

  • To connect MIDI/control-surface state to local automation behavior.
  • To discover, pair with, and control your selected local Hue Bridge.
  • To persist your app configuration between launches.
  • To troubleshoot app issues and maintain reliable local operation.

No account identity processing

TakeLight does not provide multi-user collaboration accounts. There is no TakeLight user profile service where your personal identity is published to other users.

Network Access

When Hue integration is enabled, TakeLight sends commands only to the user-selected Philips Hue Bridge on your local area network.

  • No inbound server functionality is provided by the app.
  • No TakeLight cloud account backend is required for normal operation.
  • No third-party analytics telemetry is sent by default.
If your network blocks mDNS discovery, you can manually enter your bridge host. In either case, traffic is intended for your local Hue bridge.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. Data sharing is limited to what is necessary to operate local integrations or comply with law.

  • With your Philips Hue Bridge on your local network when you enable Hue automation.
  • With Apple as required for macOS distribution, app notarization, and App Store operation.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.
  • As part of a business reorganization, merger, sale, or asset transfer if TakeLight changes ownership.
We do not share personal information with third parties for their own targeted advertising purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can choose whether to pair a Hue Bridge and which lights are controlled.
  • You can remove local configuration by clearing app settings or uninstalling the app.
  • You can revoke local network permissions in macOS settings.
  • You can contact us with questions or privacy requests.

Data retention

TakeLight stores configuration on your device for as long as needed to provide app functionality. If you uninstall the app, local app data is removed by the operating system according to macOS behavior.

Children

TakeLight is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
