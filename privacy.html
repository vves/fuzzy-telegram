Privacy policy for the LineupSync app and related invite pages.

LineupSync is an online ensemble-planning app operated by Cello Hill. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices you have when using LineupSync on mobile or opening a related invite link on the web.

Last updated: May 17, 2026
No in-app ads

Account-based service

LineupSync requires an account and stores the ensemble planning data you create so your group can collaborate.

What We Collect

We collect the information needed to run LineupSync as a collaborative planning service for ensembles, bands, and similar groups.

Account and profile information

This may include your email address, display name, instrument, proficiency details, and other profile information you choose to add. Authentication credentials are handled through our auth provider; we do not store your password in plaintext.

Ensemble and planning data

We store ensemble membership, positions, assignments, availability, session titles, dates, times, locations, RSVP responses, and related planning activity created in the app.

Invite information

Coordinators can invite members by email. We store invite email addresses, invite status, expiration details, and related ensemble metadata needed to process those invitations.

Device and operational data

We may process your mobile platform, app version, push notification token, and basic service logs such as request IDs, user agent, requested routes, and timing data for security and troubleshooting.

Information stored on your device

  • Pending invite handoff information so the app can finish an invite flow after sign-in.
  • Your preferred calendar selection when you choose where LineupSync should add events.
  • Appearance preferences such as your chosen light, dark, or system theme.

Optional permissions

  • Calendar access lets you add LineupSync sessions to your device calendar.
  • Push notification permission lets us send session and availability updates to your device.
We do not use in-app advertising SDKs, and we do not knowingly use cross-app tracking for advertising within LineupSync.

How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you into LineupSync.
  • To let ensembles manage members, roles, positions, availability, and sessions.
  • To deliver invite links, accept invitations, and connect members to the right ensemble.
  • To send optional push notifications about planning activity such as proposed or confirmed sessions.
  • To let you add sessions to your device calendar when you request that feature.
  • To maintain the service, investigate issues, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How collaboration affects visibility

LineupSync is built for shared planning. If you are an active member of an ensemble, other active members in that ensemble may be able to see the planning data needed to coordinate, including your name, role, assignments, availability, and session participation.

How We Share Information

We share information only when needed to operate LineupSync or when we are required to do so.

  • With other members of your ensemble, as described above, so collaborative planning works.
  • With infrastructure and service providers that help us run authentication, hosting, databases, storage, and notifications.
  • With Apple, Google, and related delivery services when notifications, app distribution, or platform features require it.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service.
  • As part of a business reorganization, merger, sale, or asset transfer if LineupSync changes ownership.
We do not sell your personal information. We also do not share personal information with third parties for their own targeted advertising purposes.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can update much of your profile, availability, and planning data inside the app.
  • You can disable push notifications at any time in your iOS or Android settings.
  • You can revoke calendar access in your device settings if you no longer want LineupSync to create calendar events.
  • You can sign out of the app, and you can contact us if you want to request deletion of your account or related data.

Data retention

We keep account and planning information for as long as needed to operate the service, maintain group history, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or de-identify it where reasonably practical.

Children

LineupSync is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
